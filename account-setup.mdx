Before you can build your website, you'll need a LocalSite account. This page walks you through signing up, verifying your email, and completing your business profile — the whole process takes just a couple of minutes, and you only have to do it once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Visit [app.localsite.com/signup](https://app.localsite.com/signup). Enter your email address and create a password (at least 8 characters). Click **"Create Account"** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from LocalSite. Click the link inside to activate your account and unlock full access. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Us About Your Business">
    Enter your business name, select your business type from the dropdown (restaurant, salon, contractor, and more), and add your city and phone number. LocalSite uses this information to pre-fill your website with the right content and layout suggestions right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Plan">
    Select the **free plan** to start building with no commitment, or choose a paid plan to unlock a custom domain, remove LocalSite branding, and access advanced features. You can upgrade at any time from your account dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing Back In

Once your account is set up, you can return to your site editor any time. Visit [app.localsite.com/login](https://app.localsite.com/login), enter your email and password, and click **"Sign In"**. You'll be taken straight to your dashboard where all your sites and settings are waiting.

## Password Recovery

Forgot your password? No problem. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password"** and enter your email address. LocalSite will send you a password reset link. Click the link in that email, enter a new password, and you'll be back in your account immediately. Reset links expire after 24 hours, so use it promptly.

<Warning>
  Use a business email address if possible — it helps build trust with customers who see it in your contact form and other public-facing parts of your site.
</Warning>
