Your LocalSite account settings are your central hub for keeping your profile up to date, securing your login, and controlling how LocalSite communicates with you. From a single page, you can update your name and photo, change your password, adjust email notifications, and — if you ever need to — permanently delete your account.

## Accessing Account Settings

To open your account settings, click your **avatar or initials** in the top-right corner of any LocalSite page, then select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu. All of the options described on this page live there.

## Updating Your Profile

Keep your profile current so your account stays easy to identify and your contact details stay accurate.

### Name and Profile Photo

In the **Profile** tab, click the photo circle to upload or replace your profile image. Update your display name in the **Name** field, then click **Save Changes**.

### Contact Email Address

Your contact email is also your login email. To change it, find the **Email Address** field in the **Profile** tab, enter your new address, and click **Save Changes**. You'll receive a confirmation link at the new address — click it to verify the change before it takes effect.

<Note>
  Updating your email address changes the address you use to log in to LocalSite. Make sure you have access to the new inbox before saving.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Go to **Account Settings**, then click the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the password change">
    Click **Change Password** to open the password form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your passwords">
    Type your **current password**, then enter your **new password** twice in the fields provided. Use a strong password that's at least 8 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Save**. Your new password takes effect immediately — use it the next time you log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you've forgotten your current password, log out and use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page instead.
</Tip>

## Email Notifications

LocalSite can send you email alerts for the things that matter most to your business. In the **Notifications** tab, use the toggles to turn each type of email on or off:

- **New contact form submissions** — get an email each time a visitor sends a message through your site's contact form.
- **New booking requests** — receive an alert whenever a customer requests an appointment or service (available on the Professional plan).
- **Billing receipts** — receive a confirmation email after every successful payment.
- **Product updates** — stay informed about new LocalSite features, improvements, and announcements.

Toggle any option off at any time — your changes save automatically.

## Deleting Your Account

If you need to close your LocalSite account, you can do so from **Account Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**. You'll be asked to confirm your decision by typing your email address before the deletion is processed.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. All of your sites, content, custom domain connections, and billing history will be immediately and irreversibly removed. Every site you've published through LocalSite will go offline the moment deletion is confirmed.
</Warning>

If you're on a paid plan, cancel your subscription before deleting your account to avoid being charged again on your next billing date.
