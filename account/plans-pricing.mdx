LocalSite offers three plans designed to meet your business wherever it is — whether you're building your first website or running a fully booked service business. Every plan gives you access to the core tools you need to look professional online, with paid plans unlocking features like custom domains, online booking, and advanced analytics. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

## Choosing a Plan

The right plan depends on where your business is right now. If you're just starting out and want to get a website live quickly without spending anything, the **Free** plan gives you all the essentials. If you're an established business that already has a domain name and wants a polished, fully branded site, **Starter** removes LocalSite branding and lets you connect your own domain. If you run a salon, clinic, contractor service, or any business where customers book appointments, **Professional** adds online booking along with the SEO and analytics tools you need to grow your visibility and track what's working.

## Upgrading Your Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plans & Billing">
    From your Dashboard, go to **Account → Plans & Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Find the plan you want and click **Upgrade** next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your payment details">
    Provide your credit or debit card information on the checkout screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new features activate immediately — no waiting until the next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All paid plans are billed monthly. Annual billing is available for a **20% discount** — select **Annual** on the upgrade screen before confirming your payment.
</Note>

## Downgrading or Canceling

You can downgrade or cancel your plan at any time from **Account → Plans & Billing**. Select **Change Plan** and choose a lower tier, or click **Cancel Subscription** to move back to the Free plan.

When you downgrade, your current plan stays active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. Your new, lower rate kicks in on the next billing date — you won't be charged the higher amount again.

If you cancel entirely, your site doesn't disappear. It stays live on the Free plan, which means your custom domain connection and any Professional or Starter-only features will be removed, but your site content remains intact and your free LocalSite subdomain keeps working.

<Tip>
  Before downgrading from Professional, make sure to disable or migrate any active online bookings so your customers aren't left without a way to reach you.
</Tip>
