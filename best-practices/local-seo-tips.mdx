Local SEO is simply about making sure Google knows where your business is and what you offer — so when someone nearby searches for a service like yours, your business shows up. You don't need to be a tech expert to do this well. A few focused actions, done consistently, can move your business to the top of local search results and put you in front of customers who are ready to buy.

## The Most Important Things You Can Do

Follow these steps in order. Each one builds on the last, and together they form the foundation of a strong local search presence.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Claim your Google Business Profile">
    Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and claim or create your listing. Fill in **every field** — hours, phone number, address, website URL, photos, and a clear business description. This profile is often the very first thing a potential customer sees, even before your website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use your city name in your page titles">
    Instead of a generic title like "Best Hair Salon," make it location-specific: **"Best Hair Salon in Tampa, FL."** When people search for services near them, Google matches their location to the words on your pages. The more specific you are, the better your chances of showing up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep your address consistent everywhere">
    Use the exact same name, address, and phone number format on your website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, and any other directory you're listed in. Even small differences — like "St." vs. "Street" — can confuse Google and hurt your rankings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask customers to leave Google reviews">
    Reviews are one of the biggest local ranking factors. A simple follow-up text or email asking happy customers to leave a review can double the number you receive. Make it easy by including a direct link to your Google review page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your business to free online directories">
    Sign up for the major free directories: **Yelp**, **Bing Places**, **Apple Maps**, and **TripAdvisor** (if relevant to your industry). Also look for your local Chamber of Commerce website — many offer free listings for member businesses and carry strong local authority with Google.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using LocalSite's SEO Tools

LocalSite includes built-in SEO settings so you don't need a separate plugin or tool. In your dashboard, navigate to **Settings → SEO** to access the fields for every page on your site.

For each page, fill in:

- **Page title** — include your city and main service (e.g., "Plumber in Austin, TX | Rapid Plumbing Co.")
- **Meta description** — write 1–2 sentences describing what the page is about. This often appears as the snippet under your link in Google search results.

Make sure every page on your site has a unique title and description. Leaving these fields blank means Google will guess — and it usually guesses wrong.

## How Long Does SEO Take?

SEO is a long game, and it's important to set realistic expectations so you stay consistent.

- **2–4 weeks** — You'll typically see basic improvements, like your business appearing for your exact name or address searches, and your Google Business Profile becoming more visible.
- **3–6 months** — This is when meaningful ranking gains happen for competitive search terms. Businesses that publish content, collect reviews, and maintain consistent listings steadily climb the results.

Don't let the timeline discourage you. Every action you take compounds over time, and the businesses that rank at the top of Google are almost always the ones that stayed consistent — not the ones that did everything perfectly on day one.

<Tip>
  The fastest shortcut to local visibility is a fully completed Google Business Profile with recent photos and 10+ genuine reviews. If you do nothing else this week, focus on getting those reviews.
</Tip>
