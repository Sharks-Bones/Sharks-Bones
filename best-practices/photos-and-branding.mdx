Customers form an impression of your business within seconds of landing on your website — and photos and branding are what shape that impression. A clean logo, cohesive colors, and real photos of your work tell customers you're professional and trustworthy before they've read a single word. This guide walks you through exactly what to show and how to show it.

## Photos That Work

Not all photos carry the same weight. These are the highest-impact types for local business websites:

- 📍 **Storefront or location** — Helps customers recognize your place when they arrive. Especially important for restaurants, retail shops, and salons.
- 👥 **Your team** — Shows the real people behind your business. Customers are more likely to choose a business when they feel like they already know the faces.
- 🛠️ **Work samples or products** — Demonstrates your quality before customers ever walk in the door. Show your best work front and center.
- ⭐ **Satisfied customers** — Social proof in photo form is powerful. Just make sure you get permission before posting anyone's image on your site.
- 🔄 **Before and after** — Especially effective for contractors, cleaners, lawn care services, and stylists. A side-by-side transformation is worth a thousand words.

## Photo Tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use natural lighting">
    Photos taken near windows or outdoors always look better than shots taken under indoor fluorescent lights. Natural light makes colors accurate, reduces harsh shadows, and gives your photos a clean, inviting quality. If you're shooting indoors, move near the largest window you have and shoot during daylight hours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use your phone — it's enough">
    You don't need to hire a professional photographer to get great photos. Modern smartphone cameras are more than capable of producing high-quality images for your website. Just make sure your lens is clean, you have good lighting, and you take several shots so you have options to choose from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Avoid stock photos when possible">
    Real photos of your actual business, team, and work consistently outperform generic stock images. Customers can tell the difference, and authenticity builds trust. Save stock photos as a last resort — for example, to fill a background area while you gather real images.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Minimum size: 800 x 600 pixels">
    Images smaller than 800 x 600 pixels will appear blurry or pixelated on modern screens, especially on desktops and large displays. Before uploading, check your image dimensions. Most smartphone photos are well above this threshold, but older or compressed images may not be.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Consistent Branding

Consistent branding makes your business look established and professional — and it's easier to achieve than most people think. Focus on three elements:

- 🎨 **Choose 1–2 brand colors** — Pick a primary color that represents your business and use it for buttons, links, and accents throughout your site. In the LocalSite editor, go to **Design → Colors** and enter your hex code to apply it everywhere at once. If you're not sure what colors to use, look at your logo or packaging for inspiration.

- ✏️ **Stick to one or two fonts** — Choose one font for headings and one for body text. Using too many fonts looks cluttered and unprofessional. LocalSite gives you access to 20+ Google Fonts, so you'll find something that fits your style without needing any design experience.

- 🏷️ **Use your logo everywhere** — Upload your logo in **Settings → General** and it will automatically appear in your site's header and footer. A consistent logo placement across every page reinforces your brand and helps customers remember you.

<Tip>
  If you don't have a logo yet, [Canva](https://canva.com) lets you create a simple, professional one for free in about 10 minutes. Search their templates for your business type and customize the colors and text to match your brand.
</Tip>
