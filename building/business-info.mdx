Your Business Info panel is the single source of truth for the most important details about your business. Fill it in once and LocalSite automatically populates that information across your entire site — your header, footer, Contact page, and even the metadata that search engines like Google use to find and rank your business. Keeping this information accurate and complete is one of the simplest and most impactful things you can do for your online presence.

## Opening Business Info Settings

To access your Business Info, go to your **Dashboard**, click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Business Info**. You'll see a form where you can enter and update all of your key business details at any time.

## What to Fill In

Work through each field in the Business Info form. Here's what each one is for and where it shows up on your site:

- **Business Name** — The official name of your business. Appears in the header, footer, and browser tab title on every page of your site.
- **Tagline** — A short, one-line description that captures what makes your business special (for example, *"Fresh Italian food in downtown Portland"*). Displayed beneath your business name in the header and on your homepage banner.
- **Address** — Your full street address. Used on the Contact page and powers the Google Maps widget so visitors can get directions with one tap.
- **Phone Number** — Your main business phone number. Shown in the header and footer on every page, and automatically enables click-to-call functionality for visitors on mobile devices.
- **Email Address** — Your business email. Used as the reply-to address for messages submitted through your contact form, so you can respond directly to inquiries.
- **Business Hours** — Your open and close times for each day of the week. Displayed in the footer and on your Contact page so customers always know when to reach you.
- **Social Media Links** — Add your profiles for Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), Yelp, and Google Business. LocalSite displays recognizable icons in your footer that link directly to each profile.

## Business Hours

Setting your hours accurately helps customers know when you're open and reduces missed calls and visits. Follow these steps to configure your schedule:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a day">
    Click on any day of the week in the Business Hours section to expand its time settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your open and close times">
    Use the time pickers to enter your opening and closing times for that day. If you're not open on a particular day, click the **Closed** toggle instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the same hours to every day (optional)">
    If your hours are the same every day, check the **Same hours every day** box. This applies your current schedule to all seven days at once, saving you from setting each one individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your hours">
    Click **Save** to confirm your business hours. They'll update immediately in your footer and on your Contact page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your address and phone number up to date at all times. Google and other search engines use this information to verify your business's location and display it in local search results. Inconsistent or outdated contact details can hurt your visibility and make it harder for nearby customers to find you.
</Tip>

## Where This Info Appears

Once you save your Business Info, LocalSite puts it to work automatically across your entire site:

- **Header** — Your business name, phone number, and (on some templates) your tagline appear at the top of every page.
- **Footer** — Your name, address, phone, hours, and social media icons are displayed at the bottom of every page, giving visitors a consistent way to contact or follow you.
- **Contact Page** — Your address, phone number, hours, and map widget are all pulled directly from your Business Info — no need to enter them twice.
- **SEO Metadata** — Your business name, address, and phone number are embedded in your site's metadata, helping search engines like Google connect your website to your physical location and surface it in local search results.
