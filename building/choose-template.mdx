Your website's first impression starts with the right template. LocalSite's template library offers dozens of professionally designed starting points built for real local businesses — each one thoughtfully laid out with the sections, colors, and styles that work best for your industry. Whether you run a busy restaurant, a neighborhood salon, or a home services company, there's a template ready to become yours in minutes.

## Browsing Templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and open your dashboard">
    Go to [localsite.com](https://localsite.com) and sign in to your account. You'll land on your main dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Templates in the left sidebar">
    In the left navigation panel, click **Templates** to open the full template library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by business type">
    Use the category filter bar at the top of the library to narrow your results. Choose from categories like **Restaurants**, **Retail**, **Health & Beauty**, **Home Services**, **Professional Services**, **Fitness**, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a full preview">
    Click any template thumbnail to open a live, scrollable preview of the full design — exactly as your visitors would see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the template to your site">
    When you find the one you want, click **Use This Template**. LocalSite will apply it to your site instantly and take you straight into the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Switching templates later is possible, but your text and photos will need to be re-arranged into the new layout. Take a few extra minutes to browse now and choose carefully!
</Note>

## Mobile Preview

Before you commit to any template, you can see exactly how it looks on every device. Inside the template preview, use the **Desktop** and **Mobile** toggle buttons at the top of the screen to switch views. Since most of your customers will visit your site on their phones, always check the mobile layout before making your final choice. Every LocalSite template is fully responsive — meaning it automatically adjusts to look great on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
