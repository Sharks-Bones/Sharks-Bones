Once you've chosen a template, it's time to make it yours. LocalSite's visual editor puts all of your design controls in one place — colors, fonts, banner images, and page layout — so you can build a site that truly reflects your brand. No coding, no design software, and no outside help required. Every change you make appears on screen in real time so you always know exactly what your customers will see.

## Opening the Design Editor

To get started, sign in to your LocalSite account and click **Edit Site** on your dashboard. This opens the visual editor. Your live site preview fills the right side of the screen, and your editing tools appear in the panel on the left and in the toolbar along the top.

## Changing Your Colors

Your brand colors are one of the fastest ways to make a template feel like it belongs to your business. LocalSite lets you customize every color used across your site in just a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Design panel">
    In the top toolbar of the editor, click **Design** to open the design settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand the Colors options">
    Click **Colors** in the design panel to reveal your site's full color palette — including primary, secondary, background, and text colors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your brand color">
    Click any color swatch to open the color picker. Type your brand's hex code directly into the field (for example, `#FF5733`) for an exact match, or click anywhere on the color spectrum to choose visually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. Your site preview updates in real time so you can see the new color applied instantly across every page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating Fonts

Your choice of font shapes how visitors feel about your business before they read a single word. In the Design panel, click **Fonts** to open the font selector. You can choose a separate font for your headings and for your body text, giving you full control over your site's personality. LocalSite includes 20+ Google Fonts — from clean, modern sans-serifs to classic serifs and bold display styles — so there's something to suit every type of business. Click any font name to see a live preview applied to your site, then click **Save** when you're happy with the pairing.

## Replacing Banner Images

The banner — also called the hero image — is the large photo at the top of your homepage. It's the first thing visitors see, so it's worth choosing something that makes a strong impression.

To replace your banner image, click directly on the banner area in the site preview, then click the **Replace Image** button that appears. You can upload a photo from your computer or choose one from your LocalSite media library.

<Tip>
  Use a high-quality photo of your storefront, team, or products for the best results. The recommended banner size is **1920 × 600 pixels**. Blurry or low-resolution images will appear stretched on larger screens, so aim for the highest quality photo you have.
</Tip>

## Layout Options

Beyond colors and fonts, you can also rearrange and simplify the layout of any page. In the Pages panel on the left side of the editor, each page shows a list of its sections — such as Hero, About, Services, Testimonials, and Contact.

- **Reorder sections** by clicking and dragging any section up or down in the list. Your site preview updates instantly to reflect the new order.
- **Show or hide sections** using the eye icon next to each section name. Hiding a section removes it from your live site without deleting any of the content inside — you can always turn it back on later.

<Info>
  All design changes — colors, fonts, images, and layout — are saved as drafts. Your live site won't change until you click **Publish** in the top-right corner of the editor. Feel free to experiment without worrying about your customers seeing a work in progress.
</Info>
