Live music is a regular part of the scene at Whiskey John's RestoBar. Local bands play several nights a week, turning dinner into a night out.

## What to expect

- Local and regional bands across a mix of genres
- Multiple show nights per week
- Full menu and bar service during performances
- Casual, family-friendly atmosphere

## Plan around a show

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call for the schedule">
    Ring [(317) 392-2125](tel:3173922125) to ask which bands are playing this week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrive early for a table">
    Music nights fill up. Grab a table before the set starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order dinner and settle in">
    Browse [menu highlights](/menu/highlights) and enjoy the show.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Guests consistently mention the music as a highlight of their visit. See [reviews](/experience/reviews).
</Tip>
