A contact form is one of the most valuable tools on any local business website. It gives customers a frictionless way to reach you at any hour — whether they want a quote, have a question, or are ready to book. Unlike publishing your email address directly, a contact form protects you from spam and keeps every inquiry organized in one place. LocalSite includes a built-in contact form with spam protection and instant email notifications, so you never miss a lead.

## Adding a Contact Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page where you want the form">
    In your editor, navigate to the page where the form should appear — this is usually your Contact page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Contact Form section">
    Click **"+"** to add a new section, then choose **"Contact Form"** from the section menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the default fields">
    Your form appears immediately with four default fields: **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Message**. These cover the most common needs for local businesses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the form settings">
    Click directly on the form to open its **settings panel** on the right side of the editor. From here you can customize fields, labels, and button text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customizing Form Fields

Your form isn't limited to the default fields — you can shape it to fit exactly what your business needs.

- **Add a field** — In the settings panel, click **"Add Field"** and choose from text input, phone number, email, dropdown select, or checkbox.
- **Remove a field** — Hover over any field in the settings panel and click the trash icon to delete it.
- **Reorder fields** — Drag fields up or down in the settings panel to change the order they appear on the form.
- **Mark fields as required** — Toggle the **"Required"** switch on any field to make it mandatory before the form can be submitted.

**Dropdown selects** are especially useful for service businesses. For example, you can add a dropdown labeled *"What service are you interested in?"* with options like *Haircut*, *Color*, or *Consultation*. This helps you understand each inquiry before you even reply.

## Where Messages Go

Every time a customer submits your contact form, LocalSite emails the submission directly to the address saved in your **Business Info** settings.

To check or update that address:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Business Info**.
2. Confirm the email address listed under **Notification Email** is one you check regularly.

You can also add a **second notification email** — useful if you want both yourself and a team member (like a receptionist or manager) to receive inquiries. Open the contact form's settings panel and look for **"Also notify"** to add the additional address.

<Note>
  Contact form submissions are also saved in your LocalSite dashboard under **Messages**, so you never lose a lead even if an email gets missed. Check this tab regularly as a backup to your inbox.
</Note>

## Click-to-Call Button

For mobile visitors, sometimes the fastest path is a phone call — not a form. LocalSite includes a separate **Click-to-Call button** widget you can add anywhere on your site.

To add one:

1. Click **"+"** to add a new section or block, then choose **"Click-to-Call Button"**.
2. The button automatically pulls your phone number from your **Business Info** settings.
3. On mobile, tapping the button opens the phone dialer with your number pre-filled. On desktop, it displays your number so visitors can dial manually.

You can customize the button label (e.g. *"Call Us Now"* or *"Get a Free Quote"*) and choose its color to match your brand.

<Tip>
  Add your phone number as a click-to-call button near the top of your Contact page — mobile visitors are much more likely to call than fill out a form. Making it one tap away can significantly increase the number of inquiries you receive.
</Tip>
