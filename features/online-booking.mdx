For service businesses — salons, massage therapists, personal trainers, contractors, tutors, and more — the ability to book online is no longer a luxury. Customers expect it. They're searching for you at 10 PM, and if booking an appointment means waiting until you open in the morning, many of them will move on to a competitor who lets them book right now. LocalSite's online booking feature lets you accept appointments around the clock, send automatic confirmations, and manage your schedule from one simple dashboard.

## Enabling Online Booking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Online Booking feature">
    Go to your **Dashboard** and click **"Features"** → **"Online Booking"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on">
    Toggle **"Enable Booking"** to the on position. Your booking widget is now active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your services">
    Click **"Add Service"** and enter a **name**, **duration**, and optional **price** for each service you offer. Repeat for every service you want customers to be able to book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your available hours">
    For each day of the week, set the hours you're available for appointments. Toggle off any days you don't work. You can set different hours for different days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the booking button to your site">
    Click **"Add Booking Button to Site"**. LocalSite inserts the booking widget onto your website — customers can now select a service, pick a date and time, and confirm their appointment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Online booking is available on the **Professional plan** and above. You can upgrade at any time from **Dashboard → Account → Plan**.
</Note>

## Setting Up Services

Your service list is the menu customers see before they choose a time slot. Each service has three parts:

- **Name** — What the service is called, exactly as your customers know it (e.g. *Haircut*, *Deep Tissue Massage*, *60-Minute Consultation*).
- **Duration** — How long the appointment takes (e.g. 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour). LocalSite uses this to block the right amount of time on your calendar automatically.
- **Price** *(optional)* — The cost of the service. Displaying prices upfront helps customers self-qualify and reduces no-shows.

You can add as many services as you like and reorder them from the Online Booking settings page. Customers select one service at the start of the booking flow before choosing a date and time.

## Managing Your Calendar

All bookings flow into the **Bookings tab** of your dashboard, where you have full control:

- **Confirm** a booking to send the customer a confirmation email.
- **Reschedule** an appointment by selecting a new date and time — the customer is notified automatically.
- **Cancel** an appointment if needed — again, the customer receives an automatic notification.

Your booked time slots are automatically blocked so customers can't double-book you. If you need to block off time for a personal appointment or day off, you can add a manual block directly from the Bookings tab.

## Email Notifications

When a customer books an appointment, both you and the customer receive an automatic email:

- **You** receive a notification with the customer's name, contact info, chosen service, and appointment time.
- **The customer** receives a confirmation email with the appointment details and a link to reschedule or cancel if needed.

You'll also receive a **reminder email 1 hour before each appointment** — a helpful nudge to make sure you're prepared and on time.

<Tip>
  Add a booking button to your home page hero section — it should be visible the moment someone lands on your site. Customers should be able to book without searching for it. The fewer clicks it takes, the more bookings you'll get.
</Tip>
