Photos are one of the most persuasive things on a local business website. A potential customer who has never visited your restaurant, salon, or shop is making a judgment call — and great photos tip that decision in your favor. Whether you're showing off a finished renovation, a plate of food, a fresh haircut, or your storefront, a gallery gives visitors visual proof of your quality before they ever pick up the phone. LocalSite makes it easy to upload and display your photos in a polished layout with no design experience needed.

## Adding a Gallery Section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the right page">
    In the editor, go to the page where you want the gallery to appear — this could be your Home, Portfolio, Menu, or a dedicated Gallery page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Photo Gallery section">
    Click **"+"** to add a new section and choose **"Photo Gallery"** from the section menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a layout">
    Select one of three layouts: **Grid**, **Mosaic**, or **Slideshow**. You can change this at any time without losing your photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your photos">
    Click **"Upload Photos"** to add images from your computer. You can upload multiple photos at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Uploading Photos

LocalSite's photo uploader supports both **drag-and-drop** and **click-to-browse** — just open the uploader and either drag image files from your computer directly onto the upload area, or click to open a file browser and select them.

**Accepted formats:** JPG, PNG, WebP

**Recommended minimum size:** 800 × 600 pixels. Larger is better — photos that are too small can look blurry on wide screens.

You can upload multiple photos at once. Each photo appears in your gallery in the order uploaded, and you can drag them to reorder after the fact.

<Info>
  LocalSite automatically compresses and resizes your photos for fast page loads — you don't need to edit them before uploading. Your originals are stored at full quality, while optimized versions are served to your website visitors.
</Info>

## Gallery Layouts

Choose the layout that best fits your content and the feeling you want to create:

- **Grid** — All photos are displayed as equal-size squares in a clean, uniform arrangement. This works well for product photos or any situation where consistency matters.
- **Mosaic** — Photos are displayed at varied sizes in an editorial, magazine-style arrangement. This layout adds visual interest and works beautifully for portfolios, event photos, or before-and-after collections.
- **Slideshow** — Photos are displayed one at a time and advance automatically. This is a great choice for a hero-style gallery at the top of a page, or for highlighting a small number of standout images.

You can switch layouts at any time from the gallery's settings panel on the right side of the editor — your photos stay in place.

## Adding Captions

Every photo in your gallery can have an optional caption — a short line of text that appears when a visitor hovers over or taps the photo.

To add a caption:

1. Click on the photo inside the editor.
2. A **caption field** appears in the settings panel on the right.
3. Type your caption — for example, *"Master bathroom remodel — Portland, OR"* or *"Our signature truffle pasta."*

Captions are optional, but they add context and professionalism. They're also a subtle way to include location and service keywords that help with search visibility.

<Tip>
  Use at least 6–8 photos for a gallery that looks full and polished. Show before-and-after shots, your team at work, or your best products — variety keeps visitors scrolling and gives them a well-rounded view of your business.
</Tip>
