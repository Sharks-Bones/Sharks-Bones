## How It Works

Getting your business online with LocalSite is straightforward. Follow these four steps and you'll have a live, professional website before you know it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Free Account">
    Sign up at app.localsite.com/signup in seconds — all you need is an email address and a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Template for Your Business Type">
    Browse a gallery of professionally designed templates built for restaurants, salons, shops, contractors, and more. Pick the one that fits your business best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Business Info, Photos, and Services">
    Fill in your name, address, hours, and services. Upload your own photos or choose from our free image library. Everything is point-and-click — no code required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Share Your New Site">
    Hit the green "Publish" button and your site goes live instantly. Share the link with customers, add it to your social media, and start getting found online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No coding skills required. If you can fill out a form, you can build a website with LocalSite.
</Note>
