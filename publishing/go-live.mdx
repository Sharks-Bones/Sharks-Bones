Publishing your website is the moment your business becomes visible to everyone on the internet. With LocalSite, there's no complicated setup or technical steps — when you're ready, a single click is all it takes to make your site live and shareable with customers right away.

## Publishing for the First Time

Before you hit publish, make sure your site is ready for the world to see. Once you confirm, your site is instantly accessible to anyone with the link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your business name and content">
    Make sure you've added your business name and at least one page of content — even a simple homepage with your name, services, and contact information is a great start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Publish button">
    Click the green **Publish** button in the top-right corner of the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your site URL">
    A confirmation dialog appears showing your site's URL (for example, `mybusiness.localsite.com`). Double-check that it looks right before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and go live">
    Click **Confirm & Publish**. Your site is live instantly — no waiting, no delays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before publishing, use the mobile preview to make sure everything looks great on a phone — most of your visitors will be on mobile.
</Tip>

## Your Free Site Address

Every LocalSite account comes with a free web address in the format `businessname.localsite.com`. This is your site's home on the internet from the moment you publish.

**Changing your URL slug:** If you'd like a different address, go to **Settings → Domain → Edit Slug** and type a new name. Keep it short and close to your actual business name so it's easy to remember and share.

**Want to use your own domain (like `yourbusiness.com`)?** Connecting a custom domain requires a paid plan. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing) for details, or check out the [Custom Domain guide](/publishing/custom-domain) for step-by-step instructions once you're ready to upgrade.

<Note>
  Your free LocalSite address never expires, even on the free plan. You can share it with customers and use it indefinitely at no cost.
</Note>

## Publishing Updates

Your website isn't frozen after the first publish — you can keep improving it at any time. Here's how updates work:

- **Everything you edit is saved as a draft.** Changes you make in the editor are private and won't affect your live site until you choose to publish them.
- **When you're ready to push changes live**, click the green **Publish** button again. Only then will your visitors see the updated content.
- **There's no limit to how many times you can publish.** Update your hours, swap out photos, add a new menu — publish as often as you need to.

This draft-and-publish workflow means you can make changes at your own pace without worrying that half-finished edits will show up on your live site.

## Unpublishing Your Site

If you need to take your site offline temporarily — for a major redesign, a seasonal closure, or any other reason — you can unpublish it without deleting anything.

To unpublish, go to **Dashboard → Settings → Site Status → Unpublish**. Your site will immediately go offline and visitors will see a generic "coming soon" page. All your content stays saved and ready. When you're ready to go live again, simply click **Publish** from the editor.
