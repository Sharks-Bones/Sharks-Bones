Over 60% of people who visit a local business website do so from their phone. That means the mobile experience isn't a nice-to-have — it's often the first (and only) impression a potential customer gets of your business. LocalSite's built-in mobile preview lets you check exactly how your site looks on smaller screens before you publish, so you can catch anything that needs adjusting while it's still easy to fix.

## Opening Mobile Preview

You can switch between device views at any time while editing — your changes are always saved, and switching views never affects your live site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the device icons in the toolbar">
    In the editor, look for the row of device icons in the top toolbar — you'll see a phone, a tablet, and a desktop monitor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to mobile view">
    Click the **phone icon** to see your site as it appears on a smartphone screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the tablet layout">
    Click the **tablet icon** to preview how your site looks on tablets and larger touchscreens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the desktop editor">
    Click the **desktop icon** to go back to the standard full-width editor view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Check on Mobile

Once you're in mobile preview, work through each page of your site with these key things in mind:

- **Business name and phone number are visible without scrolling.** Customers should be able to identify your business and contact you the moment they land on your page.
- **Photos are not cropped in an awkward way.** A banner photo that looks great on desktop can sometimes cut off faces or important details on mobile.
- **The navigation menu collapses into a hamburger icon.** Tap it to confirm the menu opens correctly and all your pages are listed.
- **Call-to-action buttons are easy to tap.** Buttons like "Call Now," "Book Appointment," or "Get Directions" should be large, clearly labeled, and not too close together.
- **Text is large enough to read without zooming.** If a visitor has to pinch to zoom in on your text, it's a sign the font size or layout needs adjustment.

## Making Mobile Adjustments

Some sections of your site have **mobile-specific settings** that let you fine-tune the experience without affecting the desktop layout. For example, you can choose a different crop or focal point for your banner photo when it's displayed on a phone.

To access these settings:

1. In mobile preview mode, click on any section you want to adjust.
2. Look for the **Mobile Layout** toggle in the section's settings panel on the right.
3. Enable the toggle to unlock mobile-only options for that section, such as image positioning, text size, or column stacking order.
4. Changes you make here only apply to mobile — your desktop layout stays exactly as it was.

<Info>
  All LocalSite templates are mobile-responsive by default. Mobile preview is there to help you fine-tune the experience rather than build it from scratch — in most cases, your site will already look great on mobile with little or no adjustments needed.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Test on a real phone too — once you've published your site, open the URL on your own smartphone to see exactly what your customers will see. Sometimes real-device testing catches things that even the best preview tool can miss.
</Tip>
