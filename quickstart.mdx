This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank slate to a live, professional website in under 10 minutes. No prior experience needed — just follow the steps below and your business will be online before your next cup of coffee.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [app.localsite.com/signup](https://app.localsite.com/signup), enter your email address, and choose a password. Click **"Create Account"**, then check your inbox and click the verification link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Business Template">
    After signing in, you'll see a template gallery. Browse by category — restaurant, retail, services, and more — and click any template to preview it in full. When you find the right fit, click **"Use This Template"** to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Business Info">
    Fill in your business name, address, phone number, and hours. This information populates your site automatically, so your customers always have the right details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Your Site">
    Make it yours — change colors, swap in your own photos, and edit any text on the page. Everything is point-and-click. No code, no design tools, no frustration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on Mobile">
    Click the phone icon in the toolbar to see exactly how your site looks on a smartphone. Most of your visitors will find you on their phones, so this step is worth taking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your Site">
    Ready to go live? Click the green **"Publish"** button. Your site goes live instantly at **yourname.localsite.com** and is immediately accessible to anyone online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can keep editing after publishing. Any changes you make won't appear to visitors until you click "Publish" again — so feel free to experiment.
</Note>
