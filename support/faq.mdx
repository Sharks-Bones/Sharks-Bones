Whether you're just getting started or managing a site you've had for a while, this FAQ covers the questions we hear most often from business owners like you. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out to our support team — we're happy to help.

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need any technical skills to use LocalSite?">
    No technical skills required. LocalSite is built for business owners, not web developers. If you can type and click, you have everything you need to build a professional website. Our editor uses simple drag-and-drop tools, and all design decisions — fonts, colors, layouts — are handled for you by your chosen template.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take to build a website?">
    Most businesses have a site live within 1–2 hours. If you already have your logo, a few photos, and your business details ready, you can move even faster — our quickstart guide walks you through the entire process in as little as 10 minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use LocalSite if I already have a website?">
    Yes. If your current site is outdated or hard to update, you can build a fresh site on LocalSite without disrupting anything. Your existing website stays live until you're ready to switch. Once your new LocalSite is ready, you can connect your existing domain name and point it to your new site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Yes! The Free plan lets you build a complete, fully functional website with a free LocalSite address (for example, `yourbusiness.localsite.com`). When you're ready to use your own custom domain name and unlock additional features like advanced analytics and priority support, you can upgrade to one of our paid plans at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Managing Your Site

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my template after publishing?">
    You can switch templates at any time, but keep in mind that your existing content will need to be reorganized to fit the new layout. Template changes don't delete your text or images — they simply reposition them — but it does take some manual work to get everything looking right again. We recommend choosing your template carefully upfront to avoid rework later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add my business to Google?">
    To appear in Google Search and Google Maps, claim your Google Business Profile by visiting [business.google.com](https://business.google.com). This is a free service managed directly by Google and is separate from your LocalSite website. That said, the two work perfectly alongside each other — your Google Business Profile can link directly to your LocalSite URL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can multiple people edit my website?">
    Multi-user editing is available on the **Professional plan**. Once you're on that plan, go to **Account Settings → Team** to invite collaborators by email. Each team member gets their own login so you can always see who made changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I back up my website?">
    LocalSite saves your work automatically as you edit, so you never lose changes from a forgotten save. If you'd like a full backup of your site, go to **Dashboard → Settings → Export Site** to download a copy of your content at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
